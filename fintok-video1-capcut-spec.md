# Video #1 — CapCut Edit Spec

**Video:** "$1,000 in Nvidia, 10 years ago" (Regret Meter reveal)
**Source URL:** `https://gethindsight.pages.dev/?stock=NVDA&amount=1000&currency=USD&date=2016-08-16`
**Final length:** ~14s · **Canvas:** 9:16 (1080×1920) · **Frame rate:** 30fps (60 if your recording is 60)

Brand colors to match the site (use these for text so overlays feel on-brand):
- Accent green `#4FB894` (wins / positive) · Regret red `#E5484D` · Cream text `#F0EFEC` · Dark `#14161A`

---

## STEP 0 — Capture the raw recording first

Open the URL on your phone, **before tapping Calculate**. Screen-record:
1. ~2s of the filled-in form sitting still (inputs visible, not run yet).
2. Tap **Calculate** → let the number count up and the chart draw fully.
3. Scroll to the **Regret Meter** line ("a year earlier…") and pause on it ~2s.
4. Scroll to / tap **Download image** so the result card shows.

Don't worry about timing while recording — you'll trim it on the timeline. Aim for 15–20s of raw footage.

---

## STEP 1 — Project setup in CapCut

1. New Project → tap the ratio button → set **9:16**.
2. Import your recording to the timeline.
3. Settings (top-right export gear later): **1080p, 30fps, higher bitrate** — set at export, not now.

---

## STEP 2 — The timeline (5 beats)

Trim/split (✂️) your recording so the beats land on these marks. Use **speed** (select clip → Speed → Normal) to stretch or compress: speed up dead air, keep the count-up at 1×.

| Beat | Timecode | What's on screen | Clip action |
|------|----------|------------------|-------------|
| 1 · Hook | **0.0–2.0s** | Filled form, not yet run | Trim to 2s. Freeze-frame if needed (see tip) |
| 2 · Trigger | **2.0–3.0s** | Finger taps Calculate | Keep at 1× |
| 3 · Reveal | **3.0–7.0s** | Number counts up + chart draws | **Keep 1× — do not speed up.** This is the proof |
| 4 · Twist | **7.0–11.0s** | Regret Meter line ("a year earlier…") | Slow to 0.8× for emphasis; add zoom (Step 4) |
| 5 · CTA | **11.0–14.0s** | Download-image result card | Hold on the card ~3s |

**Freeze-frame tip:** to hold the form still for the hook, select the clip → **Freeze** (snowflake icon) at frame 0, drag the frozen segment to 2s.

---

## STEP 3 — Text overlays (exact copy, font, size, position)

Add each via **Text → Add text**. For all text layers:
- **Font:** a heavy sans — CapCut *"Aa"* → pick a **Bold** display font (e.g. *"Rubik Bold"*, *"Montserrat"*-style, or the default *"Classic Bold"*). Keep the SAME font on every layer.
- **Style:** turn ON **Stroke** (black, ~8–10) OR **Background** (dark pill `#14161A`, 60–80% opacity) so text reads over the chart.
- **Alignment:** center.

| Layer | Timecode | Text (exact) | Size | Position | Color | Animation |
|-------|----------|--------------|------|----------|-------|-----------|
| **T1 · Hook** | 0.0–7.0s | `$1,000 in Nvidia` / `10 years ago 👇` | ~**14–16** (big; ~2 lines) | **Top third** (y ≈ 20%) | Cream `#F0EFEC` | In: **Slide down** · Out: none |
| **T2 · Tease** | 3.0–7.0s | `wait for it…` | ~8 (small) | Just under T1 | Muted `#93989F` | In: **Fade** · Loop: **Pulse** (subtle) |
| **T3 · Twist** | 7.2–11.0s | `and ONE year earlier? 💀` | ~**13** | **Middle** (y ≈ 45%) | Regret red `#E5484D` | In: **Pop / Bounce** · Shake (optional) |
| **T4 · CTA** | 11.0–14.0s | `Run yours FREE 👉 link in bio` | ~11 | **Bottom third** (y ≈ 80%) | Accent green `#4FB894` | In: **Slide up** |

Notes:
- **T1 stays up through the reveal** (0–7s) so the hook frames the count-up. It exits when the twist enters.
- Put a **hard line break** between "Nvidia" and "10 years ago" so it stacks cleanly.
- The 💀 on T3 is the emotional beat — make it big.

---

## STEP 4 — Punch-in zoom on the two money moments

Use **keyframes** (the ◆ diamond) on the *video clip* Scale property:

1. **On the reveal (Beat 3, ~3.0s):** keyframe Scale **100%**. At ~4.0s, keyframe Scale **115%** slowly centered on the number. Adds drama to the count-up.
2. **On the twist (Beat 4, ~7.2s):** snap-zoom — keyframe Scale **100%** then **125%** over 0.3s onto the regret line.

Keep zooms subtle; the number does the work.

---

## STEP 5 — Auto-captions (only if you add voiceover)

If you record a quick VO ("A thousand dollars in Nvidia, ten years ago…"):
- **Text → Auto captions → your language.**
- Style: same bold font, cream fill, black stroke, **bottom-center (y ≈ 70%)**, one line at a time, size ~8.
- Turn on a **word-by-word highlight** (accent green) if available.

No VO? Skip this — the on-screen text carries it.

---

## STEP 6 — Sound design

1. **Trending sound (primary):** add it in the **TikTok app when you post**, not here (licensing + algorithm). In CapCut, just rough-cut to a placeholder beat so your timing is close.
2. **SFX (add in CapCut):** Audio → Sound effects:
   - **"Whoosh"** on each text entrance (0.0s, 7.2s).
   - **"Riser / build-up"** under the count-up (3.0–7.0s).
   - **"Ding" or "cash register"** exactly on the final number landing (~6.8s).
   - **"Impact / boom"** on the twist (7.2s).
3. Duck any music under the SFX so the ding punches through.

---

## STEP 7 — Finishing touches

- **Adjust (clip):** nudge Contrast +5, Saturation +8 so the chart pops. Don't overdo it.
- **Add a 0.2s fade-in** at the very start and **fade-out** at the end (clip edges).
- **Cover/thumbnail:** set the frame showing the final number + green chart (CapCut → Edit cover).
