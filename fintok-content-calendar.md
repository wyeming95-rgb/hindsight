# Hindsight — 30-Day Fintok Content Calendar

**App:** Hindsight — the "what if I'd invested $X in ___ back in ___" historical stock calculator.

**Window:** Mon 17 Aug 2026 → Tue 15 Sep 2026 · 1 post/day · 30 posts
**Goal:** Drive clicks to the tool. Every post ends with the same ask.
**Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts (same vertical video), repurposed to X and Threads.
**Star of the show:** the calculator itself (**Hindsight**), on screen, every single post.

---

## 1. Standing setup (read once, applies to every post)

### Brand voice
Curious, a little chaotic, never preachy. You're not a financial advisor — you're the friend with a time machine who says *"wanna see something painful?"* Awe + regret + humour. Never "you should buy X." Always past-tense hypothetical: *"what if you HAD…"*

### The universal disclaimer (bake into every video)
On-screen small text + one line in caption:
> *Historical & educational only. Not financial advice. No dividends/fees/taxes. Past performance ≠ future results.*
This is on-brand, not a legal chore — it keeps the "what if" framing honest and keeps you safe.

### Video template (every post, ~15–30s)
1. **0.0–1.5s — Hook text** big on screen, one thumb-stopping number or question. No intro, no "hey guys."
2. **1.5–4s — Setup**: type the inputs into the tool on screen (ticker, amount, currency, date). Let them watch it fill in.
3. **4–8s — The reveal**: hit calculate, the number lands. Zoom/punch-in on the headline value. Let the chart draw.
4. **8–end — Twist + CTA**: the regret meter / beat-market verdict / a reaction, then the CTA.

### CTA bank (rotate — same ask, different words)
- "Run YOUR number — link in bio. It's free, no signup."
- "Type a ticker + a year in the comments, I'll do it next."
- "Try it on your own money. Link in bio. Takes 10 seconds."
- "Screenshot your result and tag me."

### Tool features to spotlight across the month
- Regret meter ("a year earlier") · Compare up to 4 · Beat-the-market verdict vs S&P 500 · MYR + multi-currency · Shareable link · Downloadable PNG result card · Free, no signup, no ads.

### Hashtag banks (mix ~4–6, rotate)
- **Core:** #whatifiinvested #hindsightapp #investing #stocks #stockmarket #compoundinterest #fintok #moneytok
  (Keep `#whatifiinvested` as the discovery tag; `#hindsightapp` is the brand tag — `#hindsight` alone is too generic/saturated to help.)
- **MYR/local:** #malaysiafinance #duditmalaysia #saham #kewanganmalaysia #ringgit
- **Ticker-specific:** #nvidia #apple #tesla #sp500 #bitcoin (only if relevant to that post)

### X / Threads repurpose rule
Post the result card PNG (the tool generates one) + the hook line + the shareable link. On X, thread the "one year earlier" twist as a reply. On Threads, end with a question to farm replies.

### Best posting times (test & adjust)
TikTok/Reels/Shorts: ~12pm & ~7pm local. X/Threads: ~9am & ~6pm. Post the video first, repurpose to text platforms within the hour.

## 4. Production checklist (per post)
- [ ] Verify the reveal number live in the tool (numbers change daily — re-check on filming day).
- [ ] Screen-record clean (hide API errors; if rate-limited, wait a minute — free tier is ~8/min).
- [ ] Hook text readable in first 1.5s; caption under the safe zone (avoid TikTok's right-side icons).
- [ ] Disclaimer overlay + caption line present.
- [ ] CTA on screen AND spoken/captioned.
- [ ] Export once, cross-post to TikTok / Reels / Shorts.
- [ ] Generate the tool's PNG result card → post to X + Threads with the shareable link.
- [ ] Reply to early comments within the first hour (algorithm boost + fuels community posts).

## 5. What to track weekly
Views, watch-through %, saves/shares (best signal for the "value" posts), profile visits, **link-in-bio clicks** (your real KPI), and which series converts best. Double down on the winning series in month 2, cut the weakest.
